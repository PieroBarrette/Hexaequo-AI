Hexaequo

An abstract strategy game for two

There is no fixed board: you build it as you play, one hexagonal tile at a time, and move two kinds of piece across it — disks, which step and jump, and rings, which leap. You win by capturing six disks or three rings, or by leaving your opponent nothing on the board.

Play face to face on one device, against the computer at four strengths, or online — with a friend through a link, or against someone of your level. It is free, carries no advertising, and needs no account unless you want a rating.
