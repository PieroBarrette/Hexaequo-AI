Hexaequo

Hexaequo is a free abstract strategy board game for two players. There is no fixed board: the players build it as they go, laying hexagonal tiles and moving two kinds of piece — disks, which step and jump, and rings, which leap. You win by capturing six enemy disks, three enemy rings, or every enemy piece left on the board.

Play against another person on the same device, against the computer, or online with a friend through a shared link. Hexaequo is free, carries no advertising, and needs no account unless you want a rating.
