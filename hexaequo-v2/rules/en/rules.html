Overview

Hexaequo is a two-player abstract strategy game. Players place their tiles and pieces, move their pieces on the tiles, and try to capture opponent pieces.

The game combines tile placement with tactical piece movement, offering deep strategic possibilities with simple rules.

▶ Game overview

Setup

Each player starts with:

  • 9 tiles
  • 6 discs
  • 3 rings

The game begins with 2 tiles of each color placed against each other with a disc at each end (matching the tile color). The player with the black pieces goes first.

▶ Initial setup

Objective

Win by capturing 6 opponent discs or 3 opponent rings or all opponent pieces on the tiles.

Note: If a player cannot play on their turn, or if a position repeats three times, the game ends in Ex Aequo!

Turn Structure

On your turn, you must perform exactly one of these actions:

  • Place a tile
  • Place a piece
  • Move a piece

Moving a Piece

A disc moves to an adjacent empty tile or can perform one or more jumps over any piece, capturing only opponent pieces.

Note: The game state must change and it is not possible to pass your turn.

Rings

A ring jumps precisely a distance of 2 tiles exactly once, capturing an opponent piece upon landing if applicable.

Note: A ring cannot end its movement on a disc or ring of its own color.
