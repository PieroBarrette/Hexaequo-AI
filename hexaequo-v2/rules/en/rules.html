Overview

Hexaequo is a strategic two-player abstract game played on a hexagonal board. Players take turns placing tiles and pieces, moving discs, and capturing opponent pieces.

The game combines territorial expansion with tactical piece movement, offering deep strategic possibilities in a elegant ruleset.

Objective

Win by capturing 6 opponent discs or 3 opponent rings.

6 Discs to capture
or
3 Rings to capture

Initial Setup

Each player starts with:

  • 9 tiles of their color
  • 6 discs (flat circular pieces)
  • 3 rings (circular outline pieces)

The game begins with 2 tiles already placed in the center (one black, one white) with one disc on each tile.

Turn Structure

On your turn, you must perform exactly one of these actions:

  1. Place a tile and optionally place a piece on it
  2. Move a disc you control

Black always plays first. Players alternate turns until one achieves victory.

Placing Tiles

When placing a tile, it must be:

  • Adjacent to at least one existing tile
  • Placed on an empty hex (no tile already there)

After placing a tile, you may immediately place one of your pieces (disc or ring) on that tile.

▶ Tile placement animation

Placing Pieces

When placing a piece (disc or ring), it must be placed on:

  • A tile you just placed this turn
  • An empty tile (no other piece on it)
Note: You cannot place pieces on tiles that were already on the board. Pieces can only be placed on freshly placed tiles.

Movement

Discs can move in a straight line in any of the 6 hexagonal directions. They slide until they hit another piece or the edge of the board.

Sliding Movement

A disc slides in a chosen direction until it reaches:

  • An occupied tile (stops on the tile before it)
  • The edge of the board (stops on the last tile)

Capturing

Capture opponent pieces by jumping over them.

Jump Capture

To capture, your disc must:

  1. Be adjacent to an opponent's piece
  2. Jump over it in a straight line
  3. Land on an empty tile immediately after

Multi-Jump

After capturing, if another capture is possible from the landing position, you may continue capturing in the same turn. Multi-jumps are optional but can be very powerful!

Rings

Rings are special pieces with unique properties:

  • Cannot move once placed
  • Cannot capture other pieces
  • Can be captured like discs (jumped over)
  • Worth more: capturing 3 rings wins the game
Strategy: Rings are powerful defensive pieces. Place them to block opponent movement or protect key positions, but be careful—they're valuable targets!

Victory Conditions

The game ends immediately when a player captures:

●●●●●●
6 opponent discs
OR
○○○
3 opponent rings

There is no draw by repetition or stalemate in Hexaequo. Play continues until one player achieves a victory condition.
